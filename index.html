About me

Science-trained.
Design-driven.

I'm Mohamed Larbi Ait Abdellah — a Senior Graphic Designer based in Algeria with an unusual background: a Master's in Applied Microbiology that taught me to treat every design problem like a research question. Precise, methodical, and always chasing the right answer.

Currently Senior Graphic Designer at Orion Lab, specializing in pharmaceutical packaging design and print production. Freelancing under @mohlaarvi for brand identity clients across Algeria, the MENA region, and internationally.

Co-founder of Increo Studio — a creative studio combining brand design with web development and AI strategy.
